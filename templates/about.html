Campus Policies & Algorithms

About CampusConnect

Digital lost and found service developed for students, faculty, and administrative staff at VSIT (Vidyalankar School of Information Technology).

Purpose & Campus Policy

Every semester, hundreds of valuable possessions—including laptops, student IDs, keys, wallets, calculators, and study materials—are misplaced across lecture halls, labs, the central library, and the cafeteria.

CampusConnect eliminates scattered bulletin board notices, fragmented messaging groups, and lost inquiry logs by offering a centralized, transparent platform where lost items can be cross-referenced, verified, and safely returned.

Transparent Smart Matching Algorithm

CampusConnect features an automated, explainable scoring engine written in Python that compares newly reported items against existing records of the opposite type.

Matching Criterion Points Awarded Logic & Description
Same Category +30 pts Exact classification match (e.g. Laptop, Mobile Phone, ID Card).
Similar Item Name +25 pts Token overlap and sequence similarity ratio >= 60%.
Same Brand +15 pts Case-insensitive manufacturer or model brand correspondence.
Same Color +10 pts Color keyword or attribute match (e.g. Navy Blue, Silver, Black).
Same or Nearby Location +10 pts Common campus landmark, department block, or laboratory.
Description Keywords +10 pts Filtered significant keyword overlap in incident notes.
Total Possible Score 100 pts Threshold for match discovery and user alert: >= 50 points
* Matches meeting or exceeding 50 points automatically transition reports to 'Possible Match' status and generate in-app notifications to both parties.

Frequently Asked Questions

Navigate to the item's detail page and click "Submit Claim". You must provide a valid ownership explanation and at least one private identifying detail (such as a lock-screen wallpaper, serial number, engraved initials, or specific contents). The campus admin will review this before authorizing pickup.

To prevent fraudulent claims, student harassment, and identity misuse, direct contact info is protected. All claims flow through the verified administrative desk.

VSIT Central Administrative Block, Room 104 (Ground Floor), Vidyalankar Educational Campus, Wadala. Operating hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM.

Campus Protocols

  • Found items of high value (laptops, phones, wallets) should be deposited at the Security / Admin desk within 24 hours.
  • Always take clear, well-lit photos when reporting items to improve matching confidence.
  • Do not disclose secret identifying marks publicly in descriptions—reserve them for verification claims.
